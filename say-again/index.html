How this works, and what it grades

The loop

  1. Brief a scenario — real field, real frequencies, random wind and ATIS
  2. Make your call, by voice or typed
  3. The controller answers over a simulated VHF channel
  4. Read back what you are required to read back
  5. You get told exactly what you missed

What counts as required

Runway assignments, hold short instructions, assigned altitudes and headings, squawk codes and frequency changes are mandatory readbacks (AIM 4-4-7). Miss one and the step is marked unsafe, not just imperfect.

Habits are flagged separately: "roger" in place of a readback, filler words, "with you", and dropping your callsign.
