Keyboard shortcuts & how to fly a Direct-To

Direct-To

  1. Press D→
  2. Small right knob spells the character under the cursor
  3. Large right knob moves to the next character
  4. ENT confirms the identifier
  5. ENT again on Activate?

The unit auto-fills the rest of the identifier from the database as you spell — Garmin calls it Spell'N'Find. Try KJYO or CSN.
